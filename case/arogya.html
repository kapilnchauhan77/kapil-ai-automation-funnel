Arogya / Aurora EHR: AI for inpatient care

A production EHR and hospital workflow platform that helps clinical and operational teams move from bedside capture to reviewed action.

Open Arogya / Aurora EHR ↗

Client
Arogya / Aurora EHR
Industry
Healthcare and hospital operations
Contribution
AI product architecture and engineering

Context

Hospital teams move among rounds, charting, diagnostics, pharmacy, bed management, billing, and patient communication. Fragmented screens add documentation and coordination work.

System and approach

The system connects the patient record with round capture, an AI scribe, grounded chart assistance, role-specific workspaces, and downstream hospital operations. AI content is advisory. No AI output reaches the chart without explicit clinician action. Deterministic rules handle safety checks such as drug interactions, allergy conflicts, and abnormal-lab flags.

  • Capture typed or dictated rounds and preserve the original source while producing editable drafts.
  • Connect charts, encounters, scheduling, diagnostics, pharmacy, bed board, billing, and patient portal.
  • Provide role-scoped, grounded assistance with source and tool context.
  • Require clinician review and approval and audit sensitive actions.

Delivered scope

Production Django/DRF and React/TypeScript system with staff, patient, and partner workflows across clinical care and hospital operations.

Outcome

Production outcome: work accelerated across hundreds of doctors' workloads. Clinical judgment and final decisions remain with treating clinicians.

Technology

  • Django
  • Django REST Framework
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • PostgreSQL
  • Celery
