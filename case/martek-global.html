Martek Global: Facilities automation

A cloud CAFM platform for triage, vendor routing, audit trails, and operations analytics

Client
Martek Global Services
Industry
Enterprise facilities management
Contribution
Solution architecture and engineering

Context

Facilities work connects incoming events to work orders, approvals, vendors and compliance documents. Teams need consistent triage and a record of how each request progresses. Routing also depends on the facility location, work category and available vendor history.

System and approach

The cloud facilities management platform captures events with category and severity information, then applies work-order templates and vendor routing. Document workflows assemble an audit trail, while Power BI exposes operational data for reporting and analysis.

  • Capture facility events with category and severity context.
  • Apply work-order templates for consistent triage.
  • Route vendors by location and category.
  • Assemble document trails and expose operations data through Power BI.

Delivered scope

The scope covers work-order automation, vendor routing, document trails and operations reporting across the facilities workflow.
